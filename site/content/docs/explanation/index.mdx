Explanation documents address an understanding-oriented mindset. They clarify the architecture, design choices, and system trade-offs that shape the DevTools codebase.

Rather than teaching initial setup or providing quick reference tables, explanations build deep mental models. They illuminate why subsystems are built the way they are and how boundaries protect stability.

## Conventions

Explanation articles follow these principles:

1. **Context and rationale**: Each article explains the historical context and engineering trade-offs behind a design decision.
2. **Visual topology**: Diagrams illustrate data flow, process boundaries, and module relationships.
3. **Alternative evaluation**: Analysis includes discarded approaches and reasons for the chosen direction.
4. **Clean boundaries**: Operational tasks link out to how-to guides, while formal schemas link to reference pages.
