## Conventions

How-to guides follow these formatting principles:

1. **Problem statement**: The opening section states the exact operational problem and expected outcome.
2. **Prerequisites**: Clear declarations of required packages, environment variables, and background processes.
3. **Action-oriented steps**: Focused instructions with imperative command headings.
4. **Verification**: Explicit commands to confirm that the operational change took effect.
